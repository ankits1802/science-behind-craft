Recorded test article on resist printing

The resist paste of lime and gum is pressed onto the cloth with a carved block, and the printed areas stay pale when the cloth goes into the indigo vat.

After the indigo bath the cloth is washed in running water, which removes the paste and shows the pattern in pale lines on a deep blue ground.

Printers say the work is slowest in the monsoon, when the cloth takes many days to dry between the printing stages.
