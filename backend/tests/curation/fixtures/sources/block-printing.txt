Recorded test source on block printing

Ajrakh is printed on both sides of the cotton cloth with carved wooden blocks.
The printers of Ajrakhpur in Kachchh moved there from Dhamadka after the earthquake of 2001.
Before printing, the cloth is washed in a solution of camel dung, soda ash and castor oil to remove starch.
The red areas are printed with an alum paste and develop their colour when the cloth is boiled with madder roots.
Iron filings fermented with jaggery for about 15 days give the black printing liquid.
The indigo dyeing is repeated until the blue is deep enough for the design.
