KalaVigyan is a research tool about the science behind Indian crafts. This notice explains what we collect when you use it, why, and what you can ask us to do.

## What we collect

- **Account details**: your name, email address, institution, the role you asked for, an optional profile link, and the date you confirmed you are 18 or older or, for a student under 18, that your parent or guardian agrees.
- **If you are a student**: your age band (under 14, 14 to 17, 18 to 22, or 23 and over) and your gender (woman, man, another identity, or prefer not to say). Your age band sets how answers are written for you: the words, the length of sentences and the way the story is told, never which facts or sources you see. Your gender is kept only so that illustrated stories can show characters you can see yourself in; it never changes facts, science or examples. No one else using KalaVigyan sees either, not your teachers and not administrators. Change them in Settings.
- **If you are under 18**: we need your parent's or guardian's agreement before you use KalaVigyan. Your institution confirms it for every student account, so no one learns your age from it.
- **If you are an artisan**: the crafts you practise, your district, the language you speak, and how you want to be credited (your full name, your first name, or "an artisan of" your craft and district). If you agree to be recorded, also the sound of what you say when you talk with KalaVigyan about your craft, a written transcript of the conversation (encrypted), and the facts drawn from it. A professor or administrator checks each fact before it is shown. Once approved, your words are shown on KalaVigyan with the credit you chose and an English translation, marked as your own account. You can see everything you contributed and take any of it back in Settings. We never ask for your phone number, address, village or identity numbers. Phone numbers, email addresses and identity numbers you say are removed before anything is stored; anything else you say, such as the name of a person or a village, is kept privately for review and never shown.
- **Sign-in records**: when you sign in or request access, a keyed one-way hash of your network address, used for rate limiting and the audit log. Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. Two-step verification secrets are stored encrypted.
- **Usage**: when an answer needs an AI model, which model served it, the tokens used and the cost, for budgeting.

We do not read your device's location, contacts or identifiers, and we use no advertising or tracking cookies. One cookie keeps you signed in.

## Why

To run your account, decide access requests, answer your questions with cited sources, keep the service within its budget, and protect it from abuse.

## What anyone can read

Craft, category, map and science pages can be read without signing in. Asking a question needs an account.

- **Field interviews from the IIT design and documentation portals**: the full transcript of each interview the portals share is published for anyone to read, credited with the name, workshop and date the portal recorded. Identity numbers, phone numbers and email addresses are removed before the text reaches KalaVigyan. When a portal deletes or withdraws an interview, it leaves KalaVigyan at the next sync.
- **Answers you share**: your answers are yours alone. If you share one version of an answer, anyone with its link can read your question, that version and its citations, and download its report. The link never shows your name, email address or role, and it leaves out passages that only signed-in researchers may read. When you stop sharing, or your account is suspended or deleted, the link stops working. A lesson pack a professor shares works the same way, with its slides and handout.

## Who processes it

- **Supabase** (database, Mumbai region) and **Amazon Web Services** (servers, file storage and email, Mumbai region).
- **Vercel** serves the website.
- **Google**, only if you choose to sign in with Google.
- AI providers (**Google Gemini**, **Anthropic**, **OpenAI**, **DeepSeek**) receive the text of a question when an answer needs a model and, for a student under 23, an instruction on how to write for them (for example 'a secondary-school student aged 14 to 17'). They receive no account details and never your gender. When an artisan is recorded, Google Gemini, Anthropic and OpenAI (never DeepSeek) also receive the transcript, to ask the next question, draw out the facts in it and translate them into English.
- **ElevenLabs** turns an artisan's speech into text and our questions into speech.

## How long we keep it

Recordings and transcripts of an artisan's conversations are kept while a fact drawn from them is waiting for review or shown, and deleted when that ends: at once when you take something back, otherwise 30 days later.

## Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or have your account deleted, and you can withdraw this consent at any time by asking for deletion. Contact the administrator who approved your access{contact}.
