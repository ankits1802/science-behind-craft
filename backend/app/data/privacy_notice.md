KalaVigyan is a research tool about the science behind Indian crafts. This notice explains what we collect when you use it, why, and what you can ask us to do.

## What we collect

- **Account details**: your name, email address, institution, the role you asked for, an optional profile link, and the date you confirmed you are 18 or older.
- **Sign-in records**: when you sign in or request access, a keyed one-way hash of your network address, used for rate limiting and the audit log. Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. Two-step verification secrets are stored encrypted.
- **Usage**: when an answer needs an AI model, which model served it, the tokens used and the cost, for budgeting.

We do not collect your location, contacts or device identifiers, and we use no advertising or tracking cookies. One cookie keeps you signed in.

## Why

To run your account, decide access requests, answer your questions with cited sources, keep the service within its budget, and protect it from abuse.

## Who processes it

- **Supabase** (database, Mumbai region) and **Amazon Web Services** (servers, file storage and email, Mumbai region).
- **Vercel** serves the website.
- **Google**, only if you choose to sign in with Google.
- AI providers (**Google Gemini**, **Anthropic**, **OpenAI**, **DeepSeek**) receive the text of a question when an answer needs a model. They receive no account details.

## Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or have your account deleted, and you can withdraw this consent at any time by asking for deletion. Contact the administrator who approved your access{contact}.
