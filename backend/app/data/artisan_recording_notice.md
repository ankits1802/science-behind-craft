When you talk with KalaVigyan about your craft:

- **What is recorded.** The sound of what you say, and a written transcript of the whole conversation. ElevenLabs turns your speech into text and our questions into speech. The transcript is encrypted; the sound is kept in our private storage in Mumbai.
- **Questions.** You can ask KalaVigyan about your craft too. It answers only from its sources and names them.
- **What happens next.** When you finish, the facts you stated are drawn from the transcript, each with your exact words and an English translation. A professor or administrator checks each one. Nothing is shown to anyone else until it is approved.
- **How you are shown.** Approved facts appear on KalaVigyan in your words, with the credit you chose, marked as your own account.
- **What we never keep.** Phone numbers, email addresses and identity numbers you say are removed before anything is stored, and a recording in which one was said is not kept. If you name other people or your village, a reviewer will hear it, and it is not shown.
- **Your choice.** Being recorded is optional. Stop at any time. Take back any fact, delete any conversation, or withdraw this agreement in Settings; withdrawing it deletes every recording and takes back every fact.
- **How long.** Recordings are kept while a fact from them is waiting for review or shown, and deleted when that ends.
