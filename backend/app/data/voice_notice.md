KalaVigyan voice lets you ask about a craft by speaking and hear an answer whose every sentence cites its sources.

- **What is sent.** Your voice goes to ElevenLabs, which turns speech into text and our answer into speech under its own privacy terms. Only the text of your question reaches our answering pipeline.
- **What is kept.** We keep the text of what you said and what we answered, encrypted, for 30 days, so you can read the call and its sources again. Email addresses and phone numbers are removed before anything is stored. We do not record audio.
- **Time.** We record how long each call lasts.
- **Your choice.** Voice is optional. End a call at any time, or ask in text instead.

To have a voice transcript deleted sooner, contact the administrator who approved your access{contact}.
