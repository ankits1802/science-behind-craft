# Craft page contract (decisions 0053 and 0081)

What `GET /api/crafts/{slug}/page` answers: everything the craft page draws, built from stored rows
only (plan `docs/plans/dataset-curation.md` section 9). Schema version 2 (decision 0081) adds
every field the redesigned page draws, so the page's structure is final at first paint: editorial
names, the GI card, dated events, environment steps, pictures by role, depth, tab flags, the
craft's richest relatives and three questions for its kind. The models are
`backend/app/research/craft_page.py` with `craft_page_process.py` (the process) and
`craft_page_context.py` (the parts that place a craft); the rows are read by
`services/craft_page_records.py` (with `craft_page_market.py`, `craft_page_offers.py` and
`craft_page_family.py`) and the page is built by `services/craft_page.py` (with
`craft_page_parts.py`, `craft_page_context.py` and `craft_page_evidence.py`).

Two golden fixtures, both reproduced byte for byte by the backend and parsed by the client:

- `shared/craft.page.golden.json`: Kutch Ajrakh as a database holding only the craft register, the
  districts and its dossier would answer it to a researcher, generated by
  `python -m app.scripts.import_curation --preview Kutch-Ajrakh --out ../shared/craft.page.golden.json`.
- `shared/craft.page.register.golden.json`: Farrukhabad Prints, a craft with no dossier, as a
  database holding the register, the districts and the Kutch Ajrakh dossier would answer it,
  generated by `python -m app.scripts.import_curation --register-preview Farrukhabad-Prints
  --with-dossier Kutch-Ajrakh --out ../shared/craft.page.register.golden.json`.

Both commands run from `backend/`. Change the models, this page, the fixtures and both sides' tests
together. The web client parses the page with `frontend/lib/craftPageContract.ts` (the new parts
with `frontend/lib/craftPageContext.ts`); its specs are `frontend/e2e/craft-page-unit.spec.ts`,
`craft-page-drawing-unit.spec.ts` and `craft-page-context-unit.spec.ts`, and the vocabularies are
pinned in `frontend/e2e/backend-vocabulary-unit.spec.ts`.

Last reviewed: 2026-11-18 (schema version 2).

## The route

| Method | Path | Auth | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/api/crafts/{slug}/page` | public reading (`api/public_deps.public_reader`) | `CraftPage` with an `ETag` (the SHA-256 of the page as served, so it changes with the curation version, any stored claim, picture or relative, and differs between the evidence and no-evidence views) and `Vary: Authorization`; a request whose `If-None-Match` names the current tag gets `304` with no body. A reader without `READ_ALL_EVIDENCE` (Researcher and above hold it) gets the page with `evidence` null, `tabs.evidence` false and only the claims and sources its parts cite (below). A MERGED slug answers with its target's page; an unknown slug is 404 `{detail}`. No model, search or HTTP call is made. |
| GET | `/api/crafts/{slug}/process` | public reading | The science process view (`docs/contracts/science.md`). No web screen calls it since decision 0071; it is kept for the voice and science callers and is marked for retirement once nothing reads it. |

## Shape conventions

camelCase JSON, unknown fields refused, ids are UUID strings, instants carry a zone
(`docs/contracts/research.md`). Every fact on the page names the claims it rests on in `claimIds`;
the page lists each such claim once under `claims`, each with its citations, and each cited source
once under `sources`. The client draws a claim chip from `claims` and its source from `sources`;
nothing else carries text it must trust.

## `CraftPage`

```
CraftPage {schemaVersion 2, craft: PageCraft, curated, curation: CurationHeader | null,
           tabs: PageTabs, depth: PageDepth, suggestedQuestions[] (three), overview: PageOverview,
           giCard: PageGi, events[PageEvent], environment[EnvironmentStep],
           sourcesDiffer[DifferRow], places[PagePlace], communities[PageCommunity],
           process: PageProcess, glossary[PageTerm], pictures[PagePicture],
           openQuestions[PageQuestion], sameFamily[PageRelative] (six at most),
           evidence: PageEvidence | null, claims[PageClaim], sources[SourceRef]}
```

- Every list is present, empty when there is nothing to show; no field the client draws is missing.
  A craft without a dossier (442 of 462 today) still gets its register facts, states, GI status,
  family and kind labels, tab flags, depth, relatives and three questions: never an empty shell.
- `curated` is true, and `curation` present, only for a craft with an imported dossier.
- `sources` are numbered by their place in the list, one numbering for the whole page: in the
  order a reader meets them (lede, key facts, pictures, local names, overview sections, GI card,
  events, environment, sources differ, open questions, glossary, the process stage by stage,
  places, communities), then the sources only All evidence cites. A reader without All evidence
  gets the same list cut at the end, so every number they see is the researcher's number too.
- The model refuses a page that names a claim it does not list, lists a claim twice, leaves a
  listed claim out of All evidence or files it twice, files a hypothesis among the facts (or a fact
  among the hypotheses), or cites a source it does not list.
- All evidence is for readers holding the capability `READ_ALL_EVIDENCE` (`core/roles.py`,
  Researcher and above; the web client's `canReadAllEvidence` mirrors it). For anyone else the
  server answers the same page with `evidence` null, `tabs.evidence` false, `claims` cut to the
  claims a shown part cites (local names, overview, key facts, GI card, events, environment,
  "Sources differ", places, communities, process, glossary, pictures, open questions) and `sources`
  to the sources those claims cite, in the page's numbering (`research/craft_page.without_evidence`).
  A page whose `evidence` is null and that lists a claim no part cites is refused. A Student's
  Kutch Ajrakh page lists 128 of the 132 claims and all 20 sources of the fixture.

| Part | Fields | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `PageCraft` | `slug, name, path, id, displayName, localName, localNames[LocalName], categoryName, subCategoryName, familyLabel, kindLabel, giStatus (GI, NON_GI, null), stateOfPractice[], stateOfOrigin[]` | `CraftRef` plus the register's facts. `displayName` is the reader's name: the register name with the corrections of `data/craft_names.json`, or for a catch-all row its kind's other name ("Other hand block printing"). `familyLabel` and `kindLabel` are the editorial labels of its category and sub-category from `data/craft_families.json` ("Textiles", "Hand block printing"); `categoryName` and `subCategoryName` stay the register's words |
| `LocalName` | `name, language, script, claimIds[] (one or more)` | a name a dossier states the craft itself is called, with the claims that say so. The list is empty on every page today: dossier schema 1 has no field for the craft's own names, and a glossary entry glosses a word (a place, a material, a paste, an etymology), it does not say the craft is called that. It fills, with no contract change, when the dossier gains that field (decision 0081) |
| `PageTabs` | `process, region, sharedScience, sources, evidence` | `region` when the craft has a cited location or a state of practice the LGD register knows; `sharedScience` when the shared-science view has a node; `sources` when the page or a region place cites a source; `evidence` for a reader holding `READ_ALL_EVIDENCE` and a page with claims. The Overview is always offered |
| `PageDepth` | `basis (CURATED, STORED_CLAIMS, REGISTER), coverage (FULL, THIN) or null, claims, sources, stages, pictures` | counts of the whole page, the same for every reader; `claims` is the number of claims the page lists with All evidence (every visible claim of the craft, its edges and its kind's concepts, each with a citation a reader may see), so it can exceed a relative card's `claims` for the same craft; `coverage` only when `basis` is `CURATED`, and a `REGISTER` page has no claims |
| `CurationHeader` | `version, curatedAt, coverage (FULL, THIN), searched[{query, site}]` | `searched` is what the curator looked for; show it for a THIN dossier |
| `PageClaim` | `id, text, level, discipline, subject: SubjectRef, provenance, sourcesDiffer, citations[Citation]` | `Citation` is the explainer's (`research/citations.py`) |
| `SubjectRef` | `id (concept id, or null), name, kind` | `kind`: `CRAFT`, `STAGE`, `MATERIAL`, `TOOL`, `PLACE`, `COMMUNITY`, `TERM`, `CONCEPT` |
| `SourceRef` | the explainer's `SourceRef` | `reliability` A to D |

`provenance` tells the reader where a claim came from: `CURATED` (imported from a dossier),
`FROM_ANSWERS` (written back by answers' live research), `SEED` (the seed import) or `RESEARCHER`.
A claim's `discipline` is the dossier's for a curated claim; for any other claim it follows its
concept's type (`craft_page.CONCEPT_TYPE_DISCIPLINES`: tradition to `OVERVIEW`, historical context
to `HISTORY`, design principle to `DESIGN`, stages and artisan actions, decisions and cues to
`PROCESS`, materials, tools and properties to `MATERIALS`, mechanisms, explanations and changes to
`CHEMISTRY`, environmental conditions to `GEOGRAPHY`, anything else `OVERVIEW`).

### Overview

```
PageOverview {lede[PageSentence], keyFacts[KeyFact], sections[OverviewSection]}
PageSentence {id, text, level (never HYPOTHESIS), claimIds[] (one or more), terms[TermSpan]}
KeyFact {label, value, claimIds[]}
OverviewSection {discipline, title, basis CURATED | STORED_CLAIMS, sentences[] (one or more)}
```

- Curated: `lede` and `sections` are the stored overview pieces (each sentence passed the answer
  engine's prose validator at import), in the dossier's section order, titled by
  `research/sections.DISCIPLINE_TITLES`; `terms` underline the dossier's glossary terms
  (`termKey` opens `GET /api/terms/{key}` or the page's own glossary entry). A sentence or key
  fact whose claims are no longer all shown is left out.
- Not curated: `lede` and `keyFacts` are empty and each discipline with facts is one
  `STORED_CLAIMS` section of up to eight of its strongest claims, each claim one sentence.
- `suggestedQuestions` are the three questions `data/craft_families.json` writes for the craft's
  kind, with its display name; a catch-all row asks its kind's own three questions, which name no
  craft ("What are the stages of hand block printing, from plain cloth to finished print?"); a
  craft without a kind gets the file's default three. Every template puts the name after "of",
  "for", "in", "into", "on" or "make" and refers back to it by no pronoun, so a plural name
  ("Farrukhabad Prints") reads as well as a singular one; the loader refuses a template that does
  not. They are prompts for Ask, not cited text.

### Placing the craft

| Part | Fields | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `PageGi` | `status, applicationNumber, certificateNumber, filedOn, registeredOn, journalIssue, proprietors[], authorisedUserCount, claimIds[]` | `status` is the register's; the record is the dossier's `market.gi` as stored in `CraftGiRecord`, shown only with claims (else every record field is null and `claimIds` empty) |
| `PageEvent` | `kind (STATED, GI_FILED, GI_REGISTERED, PRICE, WORKFORCE), start, end, date (YYYY, YYYY-MM or YYYY-MM-DD, or null), place, claimIds[] (one or more)` | `GI_FILED` and `GI_REGISTERED` cite the GI claims that state the date (all of them when none does); `PRICE` and `WORKFORCE` are the stored `CraftPrice` and `CraftWorkforce` rows; `STATED` is every other fact whose text states a year (`research/timeline.years_offered`), at its earliest span; a century range ("the 11th to 14th century", "the 18th and 19th centuries", "the late 16th or early 17th century") is one span from the first century's start to the last one's end (1001 to 1400); a temperature, a sum of money or a multiplied count ("1250-1280°C", "Rs 2000 crore", "1450 million years") is never a year; a fact that reports an absence ("no record", "no examples", "no evidence") is no event; hypotheses never; on a curated page only curated claims. Ordered by `start`, `end`, then kind |
| `EnvironmentStep` | `factor (WATER, SOIL, CLIMATE, LANDFORM, VEGETATION, OTHER), place {key, name} or null, claimIds[]` | the dossier's environment links in its order, the story of why the craft is where it is; empty without a dossier. `factor` is one of the six (`models/enums.ENVIRONMENT_FACTORS`, the dossier's vocabulary); `place` is set only when its key is in `places` |
| `PageRelative` | `craft: CraftRef, displayName, giStatus, stateOfPractice[], basis, coverage, claims, hero {media, caption} or null, lede {text, citations[], sources[]} or null` | up to six other active crafts of the same sub-category, never a catch-all row, richest first: curated (FULL before THIN), then by stored claims, then GI before not, then by name. `claims` is the craft's visible concept claims in the page's mode (`services/craft_claim_counts`, the count the catalogue's `depth.claims` shows), not the relative's own `depth.claims`. A curated relative carries the hero its own page shows (`craft_page_family.shown_pictures`: a picture with stored media and a visible claim or a stage, the first inline one of no stage leading) and the first sentence of its lede whose claims are all visible, with that sentence's own citations and sources (they are not the page's claims) |

### Facts beside the overview

| Part | Fields | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `DifferRow` | `key, about, weight (1 to 3), claimIds[] (two or more)` | "Sources differ": every claim, never resolved. `key` is `differ-<n>`, the row's place in the dossier. `weight` 3 when the row touches a stage's claims or timing, or a chemistry or physics claim; 2 for another science discipline (process, materials, geology, climate, ecology, botany, health); 1 otherwise. Rows come heaviest first, in dossier order within a weight |
| `PagePlace` | `key, name, kind, role, district {lgd, name} or null, state {lgd, name} or null, country, wikidataId, claimIds[]` | `kind` VILLAGE, TOWN, DISTRICT, STATE, COUNTRY, REGION, RIVER; `role` PRACTISED, ORIGIN, HISTORIC_CENTRE, MATERIAL_SOURCE, MARKET, MIGRATED_FROM, MIGRATED_TO; a place with two roles is listed once per role; `lgd` is null for a district the LGD register lists as pending and for places abroad. | `PageCommunity` | `key, kind (COMMUNITY, PERSON, ORGANISATION), name, localNames[], honours[], place (a place name or null), placeKey, claimIds[]` | a person only as a published source names them (decision D6), with the honours the dossier gives; `placeKey` points at the place's key in `places`, and `place` and `placeKey` are null when that place is not shown (both sides refuse a key `places` does not list) |
| `PageTerm` | `key (term key or null), label, names[], meaning, language, script, claimIds[]` | curated: the dossier's meaning and claims; not curated: the craft's glossary terms with `meaning` null and no claims (open the term route for a cited summary) |
| `PagePicture` | `media: MediaRef, caption, role (HERO, OVERVIEW, STAGE), stageId, claimIds[]` | `MediaRef` is the explainer's (its `width` and `height`, `author`, `credit`, `licence` and `licenceUrl`); draw inline pictures with author, licence and site, link cards as the explainer does. One picture at most is the `HERO`: the first inline picture of no stage, else the first inline stage picture; the rest are `STAGE` when they name a stage, else `OVERVIEW` |
| `PageQuestion` | `key, question, settledBy, claimIds[]` | the hypotheses and disputes the sources name |

### Process

```
PageProcess {processId, stages[PageStage], reactions[PageReaction]}
PageStage {id, order (null when no source states the sequence), name, localName, stageType,
           claimIds[], materials[StageItem], tools[StageItem], timing[StageTiming],
           reactions[PageReaction], differ[] (keys of sourcesDiffer rows)}
StageItem {id (Material row), name, kind, role, localNames[], botanicalName, pubchemCid, claimIds[]}
StageTiming {kind DURATION | SEASON, text (as the source states it), claimId}
PageReaction {id, kind, label, species[{role REACTANT | PRODUCT, name, formula, coefficient}],
              balanced, equation (mhchem, only when balanced), claimIds[]}
```

- Stages come in reading order: numbered stages by `order`, then the unnumbered ones. Number a
  stage only when `order` is set.
- A curated craft shows its curated process (`Process.variant` `curation`); another craft shows
  its stored process with the most stages (the seed's), with durations from stored readings.
- `process.reactions` are reactions the dossier states without a stage. A reaction that does
  not balance has no equation and is shown as text with its citations.
- A stage's `differ` names the sources-differ rows that share a claim with the stage or its
  timing, so the stage can show the disagreement where it happens.
- A material's `botanicalName` and `pubchemCid` are the dossier's; a stored material without a
  dossier carries its linked compound's CID.
- Timing is only what a source states: `DURATION` and `SEASON` with their own claim. Repeats,
  temperatures and the colour the cloth gains need dossier fields that do not exist yet.

### All evidence

Null for a reader without `READ_ALL_EVIDENCE`.

```
PageEvidence {groups[EvidenceGroup], hypotheses[EvidenceEntry]}
EvidenceGroup {discipline, title, subjects[{subject: SubjectRef, entries[EvidenceEntry]}]}
EvidenceEntry {claimId, foldedIds[]}
```

- Every listed claim appears exactly once: as an entry or folded under one. Groups follow the
  disciplines' order; subjects run craft, stages, materials, tools, places, communities, terms,
  other concepts, then by name.
- Duplicates fold by the checker's rule (`curation/duplicates.duplicate_key`, rapidfuzz `ratio`
  or `token_sort_ratio` of 90 or more), never across facts and hypotheses; the entry is the
  strongest claim: curated, then researcher, then from answers, then seed, then by level, number
  of citations and text.
- `hypotheses` stand apart, in discipline order.
